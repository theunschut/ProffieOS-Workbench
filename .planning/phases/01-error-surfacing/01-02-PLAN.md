<objective>
Deliver the user-visible error-surfacing capability on top of Plan 01's typed-exception foundation: a `FailedSetting` model, a `FailedSettings` list and `SettingsLoadFailed` event on `SaberStateService`, reactive classification in `TryLoadIntSetting`/`TryLoadBoolSetting` (unsupported → stay hidden per D-02; genuine failure → record with a specific reason per D-05), removal of the bare `catch {}` in `LoadSettingsBackgroundAsync`, the D-01 panel-level error banner with a per-item manual Retry button in `SettingsPanel`, and the D-04 global cross-page Snackbar toast subscribed once from the always-mounted `MainLayout`.

After this plan, all three ERR-01/ERR-02 Success Criteria are met: (1) a failed supported setting shows a visible error/retry indicator instead of silently vanishing; (2) the UI distinguishes firmware-unsupported (hidden) from read-failed (banner) — verifiable on the real USB board by comparing an unsupported command against a forced read failure; (3) a background load failure surfaces via an immediate app-wide toast regardless of the mounted page, instead of being swallowed by a bare `catch {}`.

Purpose: This is the phase's shippable slice — the moment a real user gains the ability to SEE a failed setting and act on it. Plan 01 made failures distinguishable internally; this plan makes them visible and actionable.

Output: modified `SettingItem.cs` (add `FailedSetting`), `SaberStateService.cs` (list + event + reactive classification), `SettingsPanel.razor(.cs)` (banner + retry), `MainLayout.razor.cs` (toast subscription).
</objective>
